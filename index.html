About Our School

Founded on values, driven by innovation, built for academic excellence.

Our History

Established in 2001, Kanpur Heritage International School was born out of a vision to provide standard quality education to the children of Kanpur. Over the past two and a half decades, we have evolved into a leading educational landmark, nurturing young minds to excel globally.

Our campus covers 5 acres of green, student-friendly landscape designed to stimulate creativity, discovery, and sportsmanship. We maintain an optimal student-teacher ratio to ensure individualized feedback and progress.

Academics at KHIS

We follow a balanced CBSE-aligned academic framework that prioritizes deep thinking.

Curriculum Structure

Pre-Primary (Nursery - Prep)

Play-way and theme-based cognitive development, focusing on motor skills, interactive learning, and language roots.

Primary School (Grades I - V)

Foundational skills in Mathematics, Sciences, English & Hindi, complemented by environmental studies and art integration.

Middle & High School (Grades VI - X)

Experiential labs, language proficiency, computing concepts, coding courses, and rigorous analytical syllabus prep.

Senior Secondary (Grades XI - XII)

Specialized streams (Science, Commerce, Humanities Stream) with focus on competitive entrance guidance and CBSE Board targets.
